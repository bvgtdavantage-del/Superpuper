# Polina — MetaGarage AI concierge

Single-page landing for MetaGarage (luxury car atelier, Al Quoz, Dubai) built around "Polina", an AI concierge modelled on the Zara page for Constant Estate.

- `index.html` is published as a claude.ai Artifact (declares the `sample` capability).
- Live answers: when the viewer is signed in to Claude, questions go to Claude (`quick` tier) with the rules and facts embedded in the page.
- Fallback: anonymous viewers, or anyone who declines Claude, get answers from the curated EN/RU knowledge base in the page (`KB`), matched by keywords.
- Hand-off: price, booking, buying and contact questions add a card with phone, WhatsApp (prefilled with the visitor's question), Instagram, map and email.
- Voice: browser speech synthesis (Listen button, Voice on/off toggle).

## Sources for the knowledge base

- metagarage.ae (EN/RU): services, FAQ, booking steps, phones, email, tuning and exhaust pages
- Chic Dubai Magazine, Nov 2025: interview with Pauline Gashkova (services list, M Monogram, 15 projects, client geography, GCC branches)

- autoevolution, autozeitung (2024): G900 (Maybach-style G 63, stock 4.0 V8, 585 PS / 850 Nm)
- dubaipicks.top listing: address and opening hours
